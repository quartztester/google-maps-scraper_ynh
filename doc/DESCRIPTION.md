Google Maps Scraper extracts business data from Google Maps: names, addresses, phone numbers, websites, emails, ratings, reviews, coordinates and 30+ other fields, as CSV or via a REST API.

The web UI lets you create scraping jobs from your browser, monitor their progress and download the results. A full OpenAPI-documented REST API is available under `/api/v1/`.

Scraping runs with a bundled headless Chromium installed on the server. Results appear after at least ~3 minutes per job (minimum runtime configured upstream).

**Important:** automated scraping of Google Maps may violate Google's Terms of Service. Use responsibly, at modest volume, and at your own risk. Heavy use from your server's IP may lead to temporary blocking by Google.
